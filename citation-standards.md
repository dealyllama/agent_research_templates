# Citation & Sourcing Standards

Apply these standards to all research template responses.

## Rules

- **Citation format**: APA 7th edition inline `(Author, Year)` with a full References block at the end
- **Source priority** (highest to lowest):
  1. Primary: peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, original studies, official datasets
  2. Secondary: review articles, meta-analyses, textbooks
  3. Tertiary: encyclopedias, explainers, news — use only when primary unavailable, flag explicitly
- **Links**: Always include a DOI (`https://doi.org/...`) or direct URL; use Google Scholar link as fallback
- **Academic journals**: Identify and name the top journals in the relevant field; pull citations from them where possible
- **Flagging**: Mark any claim without a locatable primary source as `[UNVERIFIED — no primary source found]`

## Key Academic Journal Databases

- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com) — cross-domain
- [PubMed / NCBI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov) — life sciences, medicine
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org) — physics, CS, math, economics (preprints)
- [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org) — humanities, social sciences
- [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org) — engineering, CS
- [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org) — computing
- [SSRN](https://ssrn.com) — social science, law, economics (preprints)
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org) — cross-domain
- [Proquest](https://proquest.com) - multi-disciplinary journals
- [Eric](https://eric.ed.gov/) - educational resources from the institute of education services
- [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/) - multi-disciplinary database of open access journals
- [National Agriculture Library](https://search.nal.usda.gov/discovery/search?vid=01NAL_INST:MAIN) - agricultural documents and information
- [PLOS](https://plos.org/) - multi-disciplinary database, public library of open sources
- [Project MUSE](https://muse.jhu.edu/) - humanities and social science journals and ebooks
